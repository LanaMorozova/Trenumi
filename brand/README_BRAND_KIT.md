# Trenumi Brand Kit — v0.1

Working brand assets prepared for the Trenumi fitness coaching app.

## Brand colors
- Primary background: `#0B0515`
- Primary accent: `#D9FF00`
- Text / white part of symbol: `#FFFFFF`
- Alternate lime (not the selected primary): `#A1EF00`

## Key files
- `logos/`: dark/light logo lockups, editable SVG and transparent PNG.
- `app_icons/app_icon_ios_1024.png`: 1024 × 1024 opaque app icon, square; don't pre-round corners.
- `app_icons/android_adaptive_foreground_432.png`: transparent Android adaptive foreground.
- `app_icons/android_adaptive_background_432.png`: Android adaptive background.
- `app_icons/android_monochrome_foreground_432.png`: monochrome layer.
- `splash/`: several full-screen splash images and universal splash.
- `app_store_concepts/`: sample marketing compositions, NOT verified real app screenshots.
- `brand/Trenumi_Mini_Brandbook.pdf`: mini-brandbook, usage rules and palette.
- `brand/brand_kit_overview.png`: visual preview.
- `source/`: master scalable SVGs for icon layers.

## Important
The logo symbol is redrawn from the selected raster reference rather than recovered from its original vector source. The wordmark uses a visually matched heavy sans-serif rather than an exact reconstruction of the earlier AI-generated lettering. Before a final production lock, the visual identity should be checked in Figma at 24, 48 and 1024 pixels and legally cleared for trademark/similarity.

Store preview compositions illustrate the planned product, not final UI screenshots. Replace with actual app screenshots once the app is built and before store submission.

## UI foundations — approved accent direction (v0.2)
- **Approved primary accent:** `#D9FF00` (not `#A1EF00`).
- **Base UI:** predominantly white, black and neutral gray.
- **Bottom navigation:** dark `#0B0515`, selected neon-lime pill with dark text/icon, unselected gray icon/label.
- `foundations/TREN_UX_UI_FOUNDATIONS.md`: usage guidance, accessibility constraints and IA caveats.
- `foundations/trenumi.color-tokens.json`: initial semantic tokens for Figma/development handoff.
